# App Store Apps

Apps installed from the Mac App Store, grouped into sets.

Sourced from apps in `/Applications` that have an App Store receipt. The Apple apps are the
Creator Studio editions.

## Essential Set

Installed on every Mac.

- `Keynote`: Presentations
- `Numbers`: Spreadsheets
- `Pages`: Documents
- `WhatsApp`: WhatsApp desktop app
- `Wipr`: Safari ad and tracker blocker

## Creator Set

Installed on Macs used for writing and content creation.

- `Compressor`: Encodes and exports video from Final Cut Pro and Motion
- `Final Cut Pro`: Video editing
- `Logic Pro`: Music production and audio editing
- `MainStage`: Live performance with Logic Pro instruments and effects
- `Motion`: Motion graphics and titles for Final Cut Pro
- `Pixelmator Pro`: Image editing

## Mobile Development Set

Installed on Macs used for building mobile apps.

- `Xcode`: Apple's IDE and SDKs for iOS and macOS apps
